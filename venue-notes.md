## Recommendation

**Book the Grand Hyatt Beijing.** It has the lower total drive time across all three arrival points — 118 minutes versus 133 minutes for The Peninsula Beijing, a margin of 15 minutes (Grand Hyatt is closer from every single arrival point: 5 mins from PEK, 5 mins from PKX, and 5 mins from Beijing South). The Peninsula does rate higher on Google (4.6 vs 4.3), so if the corporate rates are close and the team prefers a higher-rated property, it is a reasonable alternative at a modest 15-minute total penalty.
